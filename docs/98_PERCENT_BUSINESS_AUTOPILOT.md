# 98% Business Autopilot — Practical Contract

The realistic target is to automate roughly 98% of repetitive operational work, not 98% of judgment or legal/commercial authority. The owner keeps the small set of decisions where a wrong click or promise can create material consequences.

## Operating Chain

```text
ChatGPT Deep Research
  -> market, category, retailer, importer, and buyer hypotheses
  -> structured cited return

Hermes + repo staging
  -> validate, dedupe, score, preserve evidence, create target/case

Agent browser / Python / Playwright
  -> capture GeM, CPPP, UNGM, company, catalogue, product, and contact proof

Hermes Buyer Desk
  -> verify identity/contact scope, draft personalized introduction, create approval card

Owner gate
  -> approve / reject / ask changes for each first contact

Gmail plugin
  -> send only the approved scope and return message/thread receipt

Reply monitor
  -> correlate thread, classify reply, stop opt-outs/bounces, brief owner, draft next response

Supplier + pricing + compliance + pack agents
  -> prove supply, build cost waterfall, prepare draft compliance and quote pack

Owner gate
  -> final price, terms, classification/origin, delivery, submission, payment, DSC

Execution tracker
  -> follow-up, order, shipment, delivery, invoice, payment, win/loss, learning
```

## What Can Run Automatically

- broad cited market and target discovery;
- public GeM/CPPP/UNGM and retailer catalogue capture;
- company/contact evidence validation and dedupe;
- buyer, demand-signal, target-account, outreach, and reply ledgers;
- personalized first-contact drafts;
- approval cards and owner briefs;
- Gmail-plugin outbox preparation after approval;
- send/reply receipt ingestion;
- reply classification and opt-out/bounce stop rules;
- supplier research, quote-proof checking, pricing drafts, compliance drafts, packs, forecasts, calibration, source health, Kanban, and reporting.

## What Stays With the Owner

- first external contact and every follow-up;
- final buyer reply, quotation, price, margin exception, delivery and payment terms;
- HSN/ITC-HS, origin, legal/tax/certification claims;
- supplier commitment, PO, EMD/security/advance/payment;
- tender/RFQ upload or submission and DSC;
- negotiation exceptions, disputes, and irreversible commitments.

## Success Measures

- percentage of leads with official evidence before promotion;
- verified target-to-approved-contact rate;
- approved-contact-to-reply rate;
- positive-reply-to-qualified-opportunity rate;
- median reply-to-owner-brief latency;
- owner minutes per qualified opportunity;
- opt-out/bounce rate and zero unauthorized sends;
- supplier 5-3-2 completion rate;
- quote-to-win, gross margin, delivery, invoice, and payment-cycle outcomes;
- forecast Brier score only after at least 30 mature, time-separated outcomes.

The system should improve business throughput while making unsupported claims and unapproved actions harder—not by pretending catalogue observations are purchase orders.
